Hey There, I'm Mithun

The Man Behind The Lens

Graduate. Explorer. Photographer.

I'm Mithun — a Newcastle-based photographer with a big smile, an even bigger imagination, and a genuine love for capturing life's most unscripted moments. Born with a passion for light and storytelling, photography became my way of freezing time.

From graduation ceremonies to intimate weddings, from cultural portraits to candid lifestyle stories — I've built Mr Clicks on the belief that every frame should feel alive. My style blends photojournalism with cinematic artistry, and I'm always chasing that perfect light.

"Photography is the story I fail to put into words."

— Destin Sparks

When I'm not behind the camera, you'll find me exploring new cities, discovering incredible light in unexpected places, and building memories with the people who matter most. I want every photo I take to tell a story worth revisiting for a lifetime.
