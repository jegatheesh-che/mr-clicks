Meet Mithun — Newcastle & Worldwide
Nice to meet you!

I'm Mithun.

I'm a Newcastle-based wedding and lifestyle photographer with a passion for storytelling and a sharp eye for beauty in the real and unpolished.

My style is all about that sweet spot where documentary meets art—no awkward poses, no forced moments, just the quiet beauty of your day unfolding naturally. I blend in seamlessly—until the dance floor calls and the real magic begins.

I believe good photography doesn't just preserve a moment, it reframes it. If you're after photos that feel like you, only a little bit more cinematic—I'm your guy.
